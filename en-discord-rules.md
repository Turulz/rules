# Discord / Community rules

## 1

1.00
Our Discord server is intended to be a friendly and safe community where everyone feels welcome and comfortable. These basic rules apply to all members across text channels, voice channels and server-related private messages.

Treat every survivor with respect, whether you like them or not. Treat others as you would expect to be treated.

--

1.10
Prohibited content and behaviour

The following are not allowed on this server:

Encouraging, promoting or romanticising self-harm or suicide.

Harassment or bullying, including targeted abuse, dogpiling, stalking, doxxing or threats.

Sharing graphic violence, animal cruelty or other disturbing content.

Spamming, including mass pings, excessive emoji use, repeated messages, link spam or copypasta flooding.

Sharing, promoting or facilitating illegal activity, including scams, fraud, financial abuse, illegal drugs or weapons.

Impersonating others or using a fake profile.

Sexism, racism, homophobia or any other hateful or discriminatory behaviour.

Sharing pornographic, adult or other NSFW content.

TL;DR: Behave normally, treat others with respect and use common sense.

--

1.20
Advertising other servers, sending invite links, or promoting them is forbidden.

We do not tolerate advertising in any form. If you want to share your own content (video, artwork), you may only do so in the https://discord.com/channels/1337655121835720716/1397373748289736704 channel, and only if it genuinely adds value and is related to this community.

--

## Wrap up

If you break the rules, admins and moderators may mute or ban you at their discretion.

This is a Turul DayZ community; please share only DayZ- and TurulZ-related content on both the server and Discord. Please avoid other unrelated topics.

--

When opening a ticket, provide as much relevant information about the problem as possible.

If possible, include the time and location of the incident, as well as the name or names of the involved player(s). The more detailed the description, the more effectively we can help.

If available, attach screenshots or video recordings that show the issue. These materials help us identify and resolve problems faster and more accurately.

After you open the ticket, we will try to handle it as soon as possible. Please be patient.
