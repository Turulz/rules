# Staff Rules & Communication Guidelines

You must follow the rules of both the Discord and game servers, and use the appropriate channels to report problems or ask questions.

--

Patience and respect are basic expectations for all staff.

--

Only tag players when necessary.

--

Aim for proper spelling, and only use Caps Lock when it is appropriate.

--

If you want to join an admin meeting, always ask first. They may be discussing something important or sensitive.

--

Do not report game-related issues directly to admins. Use the ticket system instead.

--

Any information shared in private channels or internal meetings must remain confidential.

Do not share it with anyone, especially players or people outside the team.

--

Staff have no special privileges or rights in the game or the community. No exceptions. We are all equal.

--

As a staff member, you are not allowed to be part of any faction or team in the game. No exceptions.

--

As an in-game admin, you are not allowed to play on the server using that Steam account.

You must use a separate Steam account with no admin access to our game servers. Any abuse of admin rights will result in immediate removal from the TurulZ team. No exceptions.

--

Ranks and roles are tools to support the goals of the community. Do not treat them as status symbols or rewards.

--

Long inactivity will result in suspending your roles on Discord. Drop us a message when you are back.
